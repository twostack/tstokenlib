## 2. The coordinator

- [x] 2.1 Add `lib/src/shielded_pool/shielded_coordinator.dart` with `ShieldedCoordinator(config, tool, ledger, funding, store, publish, clock)`, `CoordinatorConfig` (plan, spend parameters, round deadline, padding stock, provers and timeout, fee rate and floor, deposit margin), the `CoordinatorFunding` and `CoordinatorStore` interfaces, and `ShieldedCoordinator.open` from the issuance, witness 0 and Y_0; carry over `CoordinatorClock`, `FakeClock` and the status report from the legacy coordinator; verify pool-coordinator "Fresh pool" on the test chain.
- [x] 2.2 Implement intake in the spec's order (transfer refusal, ring, nullifiers spent or pending, deposit covenant via `findDeposits` against the tip's PP3 with the margin and the receipt matched, receipt slots, balance, then `verifyProof`), returning a `PoolReply`; verify coordinator-service "Nullifier already pending", "Bad proof" (a swapped proof fails after every cheaper check, shown by counting verifications), "Bundle that does not match the proof", and pool-coordinator "The fixture's deposit", "A deposit for the previous round" and "A receipt that is not the transfer's".
- [x] 2.3 Implement closing: the deadline and full-round triggers, one build at a time, expired anchors dropped at close with a reply, padding from the stock (a `ShieldedPaddingSupply` over `ShieldedTransfer.padding`), then the build: trees copied from the ledger, header advanced, `aggregate` through the prover pool, `PoolRoundProof.root`, Y_{N+1}, the round and the witness; verify pool-coordinator "A round from the test chain's transfers" (the three transactions apply to a fresh reader and reach header 1, then round 2 reaches header 2), coordinator-service "Deadline with a short round" and "Anchor aged out while pending" (headers advanced by hand on the ledger before close), and pool-coordinator "Stock consumed" and "Padding leaves".
- [x] 2.4 Implement funding and fees: a dry build per transaction, `feeFor` at the configured rate with the floor, `funding.output(minValue)` for each of Y, the round and the witness, the value checked, and refusal before aggregation when funding is absent; verify pool-coordinator "Priced from size" (each transaction's fee is at least size × rate and the floor) and "No funding" (the pending transfers stay pending and nothing was proved).
- [x] 2.5 Implement the pre-publish apply, the store call and the publish order; verify pool-coordinator "A round the ledger refuses is not published" (a tool subclass whose witness carries the bundles swapped: the apply refuses it, the ledger is unchanged, the store is not called and nothing is published) and "Stored before published" (the store sees all three before the first publish, and publishes come Y, round, witness).
- [x] 2.6 Implement a failed round (return to pending on funding or build failure; drop with expired on an aggregation refusal) and the restart behaviour; verify coordinator-service "Funding fails at close" and "Pending lost" (a new coordinator on the same ledger has an empty pending round, and resubmitting is accepted).
- [x] 2.7 Keep idle work and the prover pool; verify coordinator-service "Stock after a round", "Nothing held while idle", "Empty pool" and "Member out" (a prover that never answers, with the timeout, as the legacy test did).
